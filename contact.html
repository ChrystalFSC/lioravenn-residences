A few things you may be wondering

Consider it
an open invitation.

Clear answers, so you can enjoy imagining what comes next.

Can I book a real viewing here?+

Lioravenn Residences is a university project reimagined as a concept development. The form lets you try the viewing journey and download a draft. It does not connect to a sales office or reserve appointments.

Which home would suit me best?+

The Solis offers one bedroom, The Luma two, and The Seren three. Use the home finder to filter by space and budget, compare plans, and save your favourites.

Can I explore the repayments first?+

Of course. Our purchase calculator lets you adjust your deposit, rate and repayment period, then compare up to three scenarios. All figures are illustrative.

Are the prices and amenities confirmed?+

Prices, imagery, layouts and amenities are presented for this simulated development. They illustrate the design concept and are not a sales offer, availability statement or construction commitment.

What happens to the details I enter?+

Your form details remain in this page while it is open. They are not submitted or saved in local storage. If you download your request, the file includes the details you chose to enter and is kept on your device.
